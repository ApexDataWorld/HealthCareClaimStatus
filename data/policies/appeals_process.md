# Appeals and Grievance Procedures

## 1. Appeal Rights Overview

### 1.1 Right to Appeal
Members have the right to appeal any claim decision made by the plan, including:
- Claim denial
- Partial payment
- Reduction in benefits
- Coverage limitation

### 1.2 Appeal Levels
- First Level: Internal review by claims department
- Second Level: Internal review by different reviewer
- Third Level: External independent medical review

## 2. First Level Appeal - Internal Review

### 2.1 Filing a First Level Appeal
First level appeals must be submitted within 180 days of the denial notice. Submit to:

Claims Appeals Department
Insurance Company
PO Box 12345
City, State 12345

Or online: www.insurancecompany.com/appeals

### 2.2 Required Information
- Claim number
- Member name and ID
- Date of service
- Provider name
- Detailed explanation of why the decision is incorrect
- Supporting documentation

### 2.3 Review Timeline
- Complete information received: Review within 10 business days
- Incomplete information: Additional 10 days to provide information
- Complex cases: Up to 30 days for thorough review

### 2.4 Appeal Decision
Written decision will include:
- Specific reason for original decision
- Policy references supporting decision
- Statement of appeal outcome
- Rights for further appeal

## 3. Second Level Appeal - Internal Review

### 3.1 When Available
If first level appeal is denied or applicant is not satisfied, may proceed to second level review.

### 3.2 Filing Second Level Appeal
Must be submitted within 30 days of first level decision.

### 3.3 Second Level Review Process
- Review conducted by different reviewer not involved in first decision
- May include medical review by physician
- Focuses on any new evidence or information presented

### 3.4 Timeline
- Review completed within 20 business days
- Complex cases: Up to 45 days

## 4. External Independent Medical Review

### 4.1 Availability
Members may request independent external review (IMR) of:
- Decisions based on medical necessity
- Decisions based on clinical appropriateness
- Coverage determinations

### 4.1 Request Requirements
- Must be submitted after internal appeals exhausted
- OR requested at time of appeal if internal process meets threshold
- Must have supporting medical evidence

### 4.3 Independent Review Organization
- Neutral, qualified medical professionals
- No financial interest in decision
- Credentials equivalent to treating provider

### 4.4 IMR Timeline
- Decision within 30 days of complete submission
- Expedited review within 5 days for urgent cases

### 4.5 IMR Decision
- Binding on plan for case-specific determination
- Does not set precedent for other similar cases
- Communicated in writing with detailed explanation

## 5. Expedited Appeal Process

### 5.1 Expedited Appeal Criteria
Expedited appeals available when delay poses serious risk to health, including:
- Emergency situations
- Ongoing treatment denial
- Significant clinical deterioration risk
- Urgent authorization requests

### 5.2 Expedited Request
- Phone: 1-800-CLAIMS (1-800-252-4657)
- Request must be documented in writing within 24 hours

### 5.3 Expedited Timeline
- Decision within 72 hours of request
- Verbal notification followed by written decision

## 6. Grievance Procedures

### 6.1 Grievances vs. Appeals
- Appeal: Formal request to reconsider claim decision
- Grievance: Complaint about service, quality, or administration

### 6.2 Grievance Examples
- Complaint about provider behavior
- Complaint about plan customer service
- Complaint about delayed claim processing
- Complaint about quality of care

### 6.3 Filing a Grievance
Submit to:
Member Services
Insurance Company
PO Box 67890
City, State 67890

Phone: 1-800-555-0123
Email: memberservices@insurancecompany.com

### 6.4 Grievance Timeline
- Acknowledgment within 5 business days
- Investigation and decision within 30 days
- Complex grievances may extend to 60 days

## 7. Appeal Standards and Evidence

### 7.1 Medical Evidence Standards
Appeals must be supported by:
- Clinical documentation from treating provider
- References to established clinical guidelines
- Peer-reviewed medical literature
- When appropriate, specialist opinion

### 7.2 New Evidence
- New medical evidence may be submitted at any appeal level
- New evidence should directly address reason for original denial
- Timeline may be extended if complex new evidence submitted

### 7.3 Burden of Proof
- Member responsible for providing evidence supporting appeal
- Plan responsible for explaining denial decision
- Evidence must be "clear and convincing" for appeal to overturn denial

## 8. Appeal Outcomes

### 8.1 Full Reversal
- Original denial overturned
- Claim processed as originally submitted
- Member reimbursed any out-of-pocket costs
- Interest applied per state law (if applicable)

### 8.2 Partial Reversal
- Claim partially approved
- Member reimbursed for approved portion
- Explanation provided for any remaining denial

### 8.3 Upheld Denial
- Original decision confirmed
- Written explanation provided
- Rights for further appeal level explained

## 9. Time Limits

### 9.1 Claim Appeals
- Must appeal within 180 days of denial notice
- Extensions available for good cause

### 9.2 First Level Review
- Must submit within 180 days of denial
- Review completed within 10-30 days

### 9.3 Second Level Review
- Must submit within 30 days of first level decision
- Review completed within 20-45 days

### 9.4 External Review
- Request must be made within 180 days of final internal decision
- IMR completed within 30 days (or 5 days expedited)

## 10. Special Circumstances

### 10.1 Oral Appeals
Members may request to discuss appeal by phone:
- Initial discussion: Support understanding of decision
- Formal oral hearing: Only after written documentation submitted
- Phone: 1-800-CLAIMS with appeal number

### 10.2 Representative Appeals
Members may designate representative (provider, advocate, attorney) to appeal on their behalf:
- Written authorization required
- Representative may submit appeal and receive communications

### 10.3 Deceased Member Appeals
- Family member may appeal on behalf of deceased member
- Estate representative may appeal for recovery
- Death certificate required

## 11. Communication and Notification

### 11.1 Appeal Correspondence
All correspondence regarding appeals should include:
- Appeal/claim number
- Member name and ID
- Specific service/item in dispute
- Clear question or request

### 11.2 Notifications
Members receive written notification of:
- Appeal acknowledgment
- Decision and reason
- Calculation of benefits if overturned
- Next appeal level rights
- Denial management program alternatives

### 11.3 Language Services
- Interpreters available in 200+ languages
- Written translations for Spanish, Mandarin, Cantonese
- Request through member services

## 12. Appeal Maintenance and Records

### 12.1 Appeal File Maintenance
Plan maintains complete appeal file including:
- Original claim and denial
- Appeal request and supporting documents
- All communications
- Medical reviewer notes
- Final decision letter

### 12.2 Appeal Records Access
Members may request copy of appeal file:
- Request to member services
- Provided within 10 business days
- May be provided electronically or by mail

### 12.3 Appeal Statistics
Plan publishes quarterly appeal statistics including:
- Number of appeals filed
- Appeal overturn rate
- Average resolution time
- Appeal category breakdown

## 13. Denial Management Programs

### 13.1 Alternatives to Appeal
Members may be offered alternative resolution:
- Additional documentation for reconsideration
- Peer-to-peer review with provider
- Temporary payment pending appeal outcome
- Downgraded benefit level negotiation

### 13.2 Emergency Payment Option
Plan may authorize payment during appeal process:
- Especially for ongoing treatments
- Payment subject to reclaim if appeal unsuccessful
- Protects member health while appeal pending

## 14. State and Federal Rights

### 14.1 External Review Rights
Members have rights under state law for:
- Independent external review
- Urgent care appeal expedited processing
- Appeal determination without unreasonable delay

### 14.2 Regulatory Complaints
If dissatisfied with appeal outcome, members may file complaint with:
- State Insurance Commissioner
- State Health Department
- Federal CMS (for federally regulated plans)

### 14.3 Legal Rights
- Right to appeal to courts if claim exceeds statutory threshold
- Right to legal representation
- Statute of limitations per state law
