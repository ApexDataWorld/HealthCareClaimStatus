# Claim Payment Policy

## 1. Claim Processing Timeline

### 1.1 Processing Standards
- Claims received with complete information will be processed within 15 business days
- Claims requiring additional information will be processed within 30 days after receiving complete information
- Emergency claims will be processed within 72 hours

### 1.2 Claim Receipt Notification
Members and providers will receive acknowledgment of claim receipt within 5 business days, including:
- Claim control number for tracking
- Expected processing date
- Contact information for questions

## 2. Benefit Determination

### 2.1 Plan Year Calculations
All benefit limits, deductibles, and out-of-pocket maximums are calculated on a calendar year basis (January 1 - December 31) unless otherwise noted.

### 2.2 Deductible Application
Deductibles must be satisfied before most benefits are paid. The following apply:
- Individual deductible: $1,000 (HMO/PPO) or $2,500 (HDHP)
- Family deductible: $2,000 (HMO/PPO) or $5,000 (HDHP)
- Only one family deductible per plan year applies regardless of number of family members
- Preventive care services are not subject to deductible

### 2.3 Copayment and Coinsurance
- Office visit copayment: $25-$40 depending on provider type
- Specialist copayment: $50
- Emergency room copayment: $250 (waived if admitted)
- Urgent care copayment: $75
- Hospital inpatient coinsurance: 20% after deductible
- Outpatient surgery coinsurance: 20% after deductible

## 3. Out-of-Pocket Maximum

### 3.1 Maximum Out-of-Pocket Limits
Once a member's out-of-pocket costs reach the annual maximum, the plan pays 100% for covered in-network services for the remainder of the plan year.

### 3.2 OOP Maximums by Plan
- HMO: $5,000 individual / $10,000 family
- PPO: $6,500 individual / $13,000 family
- HDHP: $7,050 individual / $14,100 family

### 3.3 What Counts Toward OOP
- Deductibles
- Copayments
- Coinsurance
- Out-of-pocket amounts for out-of-network care (subject to allowed amount)

### 3.4 What Doesn't Count Toward OOP
- Premiums
- Amounts above contracted/allowed amounts
- Non-covered services
- Out-of-network fees beyond allowed amounts

## 4. In-Network vs. Out-of-Network Payment

### 4.1 Allowed Amount Definition
The allowed amount is the maximum amount the plan will consider for payment. This is based on:
- Negotiated fee schedules for in-network providers
- Medicare rates (typically 120% of Medicare) for out-of-network providers

### 4.2 In-Network Benefits
- Medical services: 80% after deductible
- Mental health outpatient: 80% after deductible
- Hospitalization: 80% after deductible
- Preventive care: 100% (no deductible)

### 4.3 Out-of-Network Benefits (PPO only)
- Medical services: 60% after deductible
- Mental health outpatient: 60% after deductible
- Hospitalization: 60% after deductible
- Preventive care: 100% (no deductible) if in-network provider unavailable

### 4.4 Out-of-Network Payment Calculation
Plan pays 60% of allowed amount. Member responsible for:
- 40% coinsurance
- Any charges above the allowed amount
- Example: $1,000 service with $800 allowed amount
  - Plan pays: $800 x 60% = $480
  - Member owes: $800 x 40% = $320 + $200 (balance bill) = $520

## 5. Preventive Care

### 5.1 Preventive Care Coverage
Certain preventive care services are covered at 100% with no deductible or copayment when using in-network providers.

### 5.2 Covered Preventive Services
- Annual wellness visit
- Preventive screenings (age and risk-appropriate)
- Immunizations per CDC recommendations
- Mammography
- Colonoscopy
- Blood pressure screening
- Cholesterol screening
- Diabetes screening

### 5.3 Limitations
Preventive care must be rendered as a preventive visit. If converted to sick visit, normal copayment/coinsurance applies.

## 6. Specialist Care

### 6.1 Referral Requirements
HMO members require a referral from their primary care physician to see a specialist. PPO members may self-refer.

### 6.2 Referral Validity
Referrals are valid for specified duration (typically 12 months) and may specify number of visits authorized.

### 6.3 Specialist Benefit Levels
Specialist services are covered at the same benefit level as primary care:
- HMO: $50 copayment per visit
- PPO: 80% after deductible (in-network); 60% (out-of-network)
- HDHP: Subject to deductible, then 80%

## 7. Emergency Care

### 7.1 Emergency Definition
An emergency is a medical condition manifesting itself by acute symptoms of sufficient severity that a prudent person would reasonably believe the condition requires immediate medical care.

### 7.2 Emergency Coverage
Emergency services are covered at the plan's highest benefit level regardless of provider network status:
- Emergency room visit: $250 copayment (waived if admitted)
- Ambulance services: Covered in full for medically necessary transport
- Emergency surgery: 80% after deductible

### 7.3 Emergency Authorization Waiver
Prior authorization is waived for genuine emergencies. Authorization must be obtained within 24 hours of emergency admission or services may be denied.

## 8. Prescription Drug Coverage

### 8.1 Formulary
The plan covers FDA-approved prescription medications on a preferred drug list (formulary). Non-formulary medications may require prior authorization.

### 8.2 Drug Tiers
- Tier 1 (Generic): $10 copayment
- Tier 2 (Preferred Brand): $40 copayment
- Tier 3 (Non-Preferred Brand): $75 copayment
- Tier 4 (Specialty): 20% coinsurance

### 8.3 Prior Authorization for Specialty Drugs
Specialty medications (typically $500+ per month) require prior authorization to demonstrate medical necessity.

## 9. Mental Health and Substance Abuse

### 9.1 Coverage
Mental health and substance abuse treatment services are covered at the same level as physical health services:
- Outpatient therapy: $40 copayment (HMO) or 80% (PPO)
- Inpatient treatment: 80% after deductible
- Crisis/Emergency: Same as medical emergency

### 9.2 Limits
- Outpatient visits: Unlimited for medical necessity
- Inpatient days: Limited based on medical necessity review
- Telehealth mental health: Available with reduced copayment ($25)

## 10. Maternity and Pregnancy

### 10.1 Prenatal Care
- Office visits: Regular copayment applies
- Prenatal testing: Subject to deductible and coinsurance
- Preventive prenatal care: 100% if provided as preventive service

### 10.2 Delivery and Hospitalization
- Hospital delivery: 80% after deductible
- Maternal hospital stay: Covered under inpatient benefits
- Obstetric complications: Same as medical treatment

### 10.3 Newborn Care
- Newborn care during mother's hospital stay: Included
- Newborn screening and testing: Covered in full
- Newborn immunizations: 100% coverage

## 11. Durable Medical Equipment

### 11.1 DME Coverage
Durable medical equipment is covered when medically necessary and prescribed by a physician.

### 11.2 DME Examples
- Wheelchairs and mobility devices
- CPAP machines for sleep apnea
- Diabetic testing supplies
- Crutches and canes
- Hospital beds

### 11.3 DME Benefit Level
- Purchase: 80% after deductible (up to equipment allowance)
- Rental: 80% after deductible

### 11.4 Prior Authorization
DME items exceeding $1,000 require prior authorization before purchase.

## 12. Rehabilitation Services

### 12.1 Physical Therapy
- Visits per year: 30 visit limit
- Benefit level: 80% after deductible (HMO/PPO); Subject to deductible, then 80% (HDHP)
- Prior authorization: Required for visits beyond 12 in a 30-day period

### 12.2 Occupational Therapy
- Same limits and benefit levels as physical therapy
- Integrated with PT toward 30-visit annual limit

### 12.3 Speech Therapy
- Visits per year: 30 visit limit
- Same benefit levels as physical therapy

## 13. Home Health Care

### 13.1 Coverage Criteria
Home health services are covered when:
- Medically necessary for treatment of injury or illness
- Prescribed by attending physician
- Patient cannot attend outpatient services
- Appropriate for home-based care

### 13.2 Covered Services
- Skilled nursing care
- Physical therapy
- Occupational therapy
- Speech therapy
- Home health aide services

### 13.3 Benefit Level
Home health services: 80% after deductible

## 14. Payment Methods and Coordination

### 14.1 Direct Payment to Providers
In-network providers typically accept payment directly from the plan for covered services and copayment/coinsurance from members.

### 14.2 Reimbursement to Members
Out-of-network claims are reimbursed to members or providers at the allowed amount percentage.

### 14.3 Coordination of Benefits
When members have coverage through multiple plans, benefits are coordinated to prevent duplicate payments. Benefits from all plans combined will not exceed 100% of the allowed amount.

## 15. Claim Payment Status Tracking

### 15.1 Online Portal
Members can track claim status through the member portal:
- View submitted claims
- Track claim status (received, processing, paid)
- View explanation of benefits
- Download claim documents

### 15.2 Explanation of Benefits
EOB is sent for all claims within 30 days of payment/denial, including:
- Claim details
- Amounts billed
- Allowed amounts
- Plan payment
- Member responsibility
- Appeal instructions (if applicable)
