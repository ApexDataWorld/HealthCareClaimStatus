# Network Provider Policy

## 1. Network Overview

### 1.1 Definition
A network provider is a healthcare provider who has entered into a contract with the health plan to provide services at negotiated rates.

### 1.2 Network Types
- HMO Network: Closed network, emergency coverage only for out-of-network
- PPO Network: Open network, out-of-network allowed at reduced benefit
- HDHP Network: Closed network similar to HMO with higher deductibles

## 2. In-Network Benefits

### 2.1 Benefit Levels for In-Network Care
- Deductible: $1,000 individual / $2,000 family (HMO/PPO)
- Copayment: $25-$50 depending on service type
- Coinsurance: 20% (HMO/PPO); 30% (HDHP)
- Out-of-pocket maximum: $5,000-$7,050 individual

### 2.2 Preventive Care
- Annual wellness visit: 100% with no copayment
- Age-appropriate screenings: 100% with no deductible
- Immunizations: 100% with no deductible

### 2.3 Emergency Care
- Emergency room: $250 copayment (waived if admitted)
- Ambulance: Covered in full
- Emergency surgery: 80% after deductible

## 3. Out-of-Network Benefits (PPO Only)

### 3.1 Out-of-Network Benefit Levels
- Deductible: $2,000 individual / $4,000 family
- Coinsurance: 40% (member pays 40%, plan pays 60%)
- Out-of-pocket maximum: $6,500-$10,000 individual

### 3.2 Allowed Amount Calculation
- Typically 120% of Medicare allowable amount
- May vary by geographic area and service type
- Member responsible for any charges exceeding allowed amount (balance bill)

### 3.3 Out-of-Network Claim Example
- Billed amount: $1,500
- Plan allowed amount: $800
- Plan pays 60%: $800 x 60% = $480
- Member owes: $800 x 40% = $320 + $700 (balance bill) = $1,020

## 4. Network Provider Directories

### 4.1 Provider Directory Access
Directories available through:
- Online member portal: www.insurancecompany.com/findaprovider
- Mobile app: Insurance Company mobile application
- Phone: 1-800-555-0123
- Print: Available by mail upon request

### 4.2 Directory Information
- Provider name and specialty
- Office locations and hours
- Languages spoken
- Hospital affiliations
- Board certifications
- Patient ratings and reviews

### 4.3 Directory Accuracy
- Updated monthly
- Members should verify current network status
- Plan responsible for accuracy of online directories
- Report errors to member services

## 5. Primary Care Physician (HMO)

### 5.1 PCP Selection Requirement
HMO members must select a primary care physician:
- Coordinates all care
- Provides preventive care
- Issues referrals for specialists
- Manages prescription medications

### 5.2 PCP Change
- Member may change PCP anytime
- Effective date: As specified (typically next month)
- Termination: Plan may terminate PCP relationship with 30-day notice

### 5.3 PCP Responsibilities
- Annual wellness visit
- Management of chronic conditions
- Preventive care screening
- Coordination with specialists
- Prescription management

## 6. Specialist Referrals (HMO)

### 6.1 Referral Requirement
- All specialist care requires PCP referral
- Exceptions: Emergency services, obstetrics (women can self-refer)
- Referrals typically valid 12 months

### 6.2 Referral Process
- Member requests referral from PCP
- PCP submits referral electronically
- Specialist notified of authorization
- Member receives referral confirmation

### 6.3 Claims Without Referral
- Claims submitted without valid referral will be denied
- Member responsible for full bill
- Appeal available if referral appropriateness questioned

## 7. Hospital Network

### 7.1 Contracted Hospitals
- Plan contracts with primary and secondary hospitals
- Member may use any contracted hospital
- Emergency: Any hospital when necessary

### 7.2 Hospital Benefits
- Inpatient hospital stays: 80% after deductible
- Hospital-based outpatient services: Same as outpatient benefits
- Emergency room: $250 copayment (waived if admitted)

### 7.3 Hospital Selection
- HMO members: Must use in-network hospitals (except emergency)
- PPO members: May use out-of-network hospitals at reduced benefit

## 8. Pharmacy Network

### 8.1 Pharmacy Coverage
- All contracted pharmacies in network accepted
- Mail order pharmacy for maintenance medications
- Specialty pharmacy for high-cost drugs

### 8.2 Pharmacy Locations
- Retail pharmacies: 24-hour chains and local pharmacies
- Mail order: 30-day and 90-day supplies
- Specialty: For oncology, biologic, and immunotherapy drugs

### 8.3 Prescription Coverage
- Generic drugs: Tier 1 ($10 copayment)
- Preferred brands: Tier 2 ($40 copayment)
- Non-preferred brands: Tier 3 ($75 copayment)
- Specialty: Tier 4 (20% coinsurance)

## 9. Behavioral Health Network

### 9.1 Mental Health Providers
- Network of licensed psychiatrists and psychologists
- Community mental health centers
- Telehealth mental health services

### 9.2 Mental Health Benefits
- Outpatient therapy: $40 copayment per visit
- Psychiatric medication management: $50 copayment
- Inpatient psychiatric: 80% after deductible
- Crisis services: 100% copayment waived

### 9.3 Substance Abuse Services
- Inpatient rehab: 80% after deductible
- Outpatient rehab: $40 copayment per visit
- Medication-assisted treatment: Covered

## 10. Provider Contract Termination

### 10.1 Plan Termination of Provider
Plan may terminate provider contract with:
- 30-day notice for HMO
- 60-day notice for PPO (when possible)
- Reasons: Quality issues, fraud, non-compliance

### 10.2 Member Protections
- Notification of provider termination within 30 days
- Continuation of treatment for 30-60 days with in-network copayment
- Time to transition to new provider
- Appeal of termination available

### 10.3 Provider Termination of Plan
- Provider may terminate contract with 90-day notice
- Members notified within 30 days
- Alternative providers identified
- In-network benefits continue through transition period

## 11. Balance Billing Protections

### 11.1 In-Network Balance Billing
- In-network providers cannot balance bill for covered services
- Exception: Non-covered services or copayment/coinsurance

### 11.2 Out-of-Network Balance Billing
- Member may be balance billed for amount above allowed amount
- Does not apply to emergency services (emergency balance bill protections apply)
- Member may request provider accept allowed amount

### 11.3 Balance Bill Dispute
- Members may file complaint about improper balance billing
- Plan will investigate and take action against provider
- Members may be reimbursed if overpaid

## 12. Network Adequacy Standards

### 12.1 Provider Availability
Plan maintains network with:
- Primary care physicians: 1 per 1,000 members in service area
- Specialists: Appropriate ratio for plan size
- Hospitals: Minimum 1 in service area or 15-minute drive time
- After-hours coverage: 24/7 availability

### 12.2 Appointment Availability
- New patient: Seen within 10 business days
- Established patients: Routine within 15 business days
- Urgent care: Within 24-48 hours
- Emergency: 24/7 availability

### 12.3 Network Adequacy Monitoring
- Quarterly review of network adequacy
- Member complaints tracked and addressed
- Annual network analysis and reporting
- Network expansion based on member needs

## 13. In-Network Billing

### 13.1 Contracted Rates
- Rates established through provider contracts
- Rates vary by service and market
- Members pay copayment/coinsurance for in-network services
- Plan pays remainder to provider

### 13.2 Billing Requirements
- Claims submitted electronically by provider
- Paper claims accepted if necessary
- Claims billed within 12 months of service date
- Timely payment required from plan (per state law)

### 13.3 Billing Disputes
- Provider may dispute claim payment
- Member may dispute charges or coverage
- Appeals process available for both

## 14. Network Transition Provisions

### 14.1 Newly Added Providers
- New providers automatically in-network when contract begins
- Member notification sent within 30 days
- New patient appointments prioritized

### 14.2 Network Changes
- Network updates published monthly
- Online directory updated continuously
- Major network changes announced in advance

### 14.3 Member Notice
- Network changes affecting member's current provider communicated within 30 days
- Alternative provider options provided
- Appeals available if network change causes disruption
